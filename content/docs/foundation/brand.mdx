## Naming

Always write **NarakUI** as a single word with a capital "N" and "UI".

Treat specific product features and modules as proper nouns and capitalize them accordingly (for example, **NarakUI Components** or **NarakUI Registry**).

## Isotype

The standalone NarakUI symbol is a core brand identifier and must always be kept in its original design and proportions.

## Wordmark
